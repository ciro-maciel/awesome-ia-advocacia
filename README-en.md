## AI Rules in Law and the Judiciary

What the OAB, CNJ and ANPD have already published.

- [Código de Ética e Disciplina da OAB](https://go.mymarketing.click/iaadv-codigo-de-etica-e-disciplina-da-oa) - The code that governs confidentiality, advertising and conduct for lawyers. `official` `free`
- [Provimento 205/2021 da OAB](https://go.mymarketing.click/iaadv-provimento-205-2021-da-oab) - The rules on advertising and information for the legal profession. `official` `free`
- [Recomendação 001/2024 do CFOAB](https://go.mymarketing.click/iaadv-recomendacao-001-2024-do-cfoab) - The Federal Council of the OAB's recommendation on the use of AI in law practice. `official` `free`
- [Resolução CD/ANPD 19/2024](https://go.mymarketing.click/iaadv-resolucao-cd-anpd-19-2024) - The international transfer of personal data, which happens when you paste data into a foreign service. `official` `free`
- [Resolução CNJ 615/2025](https://go.mymarketing.click/iaadv-resolucao-cnj-615-2025) - The rules for the use of AI in the Judiciary. `official` `free`

## Official Sources to Check

Where to check the law, the precedent summary and the court notice before using what the AI wrote.

- [Consolidação das Leis do Trabalho](https://go.mymarketing.click/iaadv-consolidacao-das-leis-do-trabalho) - The CLT on the Planalto site. `official` `free`
- [Constituição Federal](https://go.mymarketing.click/iaadv-constituicao-federal) - The Constitution, compiled on the Planalto site. `official` `free`
- [Consulta do DJEN](https://go.mymarketing.click/iaadv-consulta-do-djen) - The public search of the National Electronic Court Gazette (DJEN), by name and OAB number. `official` `free`
- [Código Civil](https://go.mymarketing.click/iaadv-codigo-civil) - The Civil Code, compiled on the Planalto site. `official` `free`
- [Código de Defesa do Consumidor](https://go.mymarketing.click/iaadv-codigo-de-defesa-do-consumidor) - The CDC (Consumer Protection Code), compiled on the Planalto site. `official` `free`
- [Código de Processo Civil](https://go.mymarketing.click/iaadv-codigo-de-processo-civil) - The CPC (Code of Civil Procedure) on the Planalto site. `official` `free`
- [Estatuto da Advocacia](https://go.mymarketing.click/iaadv-estatuto-da-advocacia) - Law 8.906/1994 on the Planalto site. `official` `free`
- [Resolução CNJ 455/2022](https://go.mymarketing.click/iaadv-resolucao-cnj-455-2022) - The rules of the DJEN and the Electronic Judicial Domicile. `official` `free`
- [Súmulas vinculantes do STF](https://go.mymarketing.click/iaadv-sumulas-vinculantes-do-stf) - The search for binding precedents on the STF portal. `official` `free`

## Data Protection

The law and the authority that apply to client and student data.

- [LGPD](https://go.mymarketing.click/iaadv-lgpd) - The General Data Protection Law, in the compiled text on the Planalto site. `official` `free`
- [ANPD](https://go.mymarketing.click/iaadv-anpd) - The National Data Protection Authority, with guides and regulations for the LGPD. `official` `free`

## Tags

Each item says what it is. The tags next to it help you choose: official when it comes from the body that sets the standard, open source when the code is published, free or paid based on how it is used, and API and SDK based on how you integrate. Items tagged "by the author" are made by Ciro Cesar Maciel and follow the same rules as all the others.
